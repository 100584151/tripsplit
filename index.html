❓ FAQ

Questions, answered

How does TripSplit calculate the price?

We add up a recommended hotel (shared rooms for groups), return transport per person, and an average daily spend on activities and food for the destination. The total is for the whole group.

Do I book through TripSplit?

No. We plan the trip and link you to trusted partner sites like Booking.com and GetYourGuide to book. Sometimes we earn a small commission, at no extra cost to you.

Is TripSplit really free?

Yes. Searching by budget, basic itineraries and partner links are free forever. Premium adds extras like unlimited changes, price alerts, offline access and a group expense tracker.

How does splitting the cost work?

Every plan has a "Split the Cost" box. Pick how many people are going and we divide the total so everyone knows their share before booking.
